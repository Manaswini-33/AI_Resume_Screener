1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CORE PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Traditional Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) and simple AI resume screeners rely
on naive keyword matching or single-value vector cosine similarity. They are easily
gamed by keyword stuffing, fail to verify if a candidate actually possesses the
skills they list, expose recruiters to demographic bias (PII leakage), and act
as "black box" scoring engines without human-understandable explanation.

HireSense replaces the naive pipeline:
    Resume ──► Cosine Similarity ──► Single Score
with a 14-stage evidence-based intelligence pipeline:
    Resume ──► Parse ──► Quality Validation ──► Anonymize ──► Section Detect ──►
    Extract & Normalize Skills ──► Evidence Verification ──► JD Intelligence ──►
    Section Semantic Embeddings ──► Multi-Factor Signals ──► Supervised XGBoost ──►
    Skill Gap Analysis ──► SHAP Explainability ──► Fairness & Robustness Auditing ──►
    Human-in-the-Loop Recommendation.

2. UNIQUE POINTS OF HIRESENSE & KEY INNOVATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[1] EVIDENCE-BASED SKILL VERIFICATION ENGINE (Contextual Proof vs. Mention)
    - Problem in Existing Systems: Traditional ATS treats a resume listing 20 skills
      in a bulleted list identically to a candidate who actually built production models.
    - HireSense Innovation: Evaluates contextual evidence levels for every skill:
        * VERY_STRONG: Skill in PROJECTS/EXPERIENCE + Action Verb + Quantifiable Metric.
        * STRONG: Skill in PROJECTS/EXPERIENCE + Action Verb OR Metric.
        * MODERATE: Skill mentioned in PROJECTS/EXPERIENCE without metrics.
        * WEAK: Skill listed only in SKILLS or GENERAL list without context.

[2] ADVERSARIAL KEYWORD STUFFING RESILIENCE
    - Problem in Existing Systems: Candidates manipulate screeners by pasting invisible
      white text or repeating keywords 30 times to get a 99% match.
    - HireSense Innovation: Computes unique-word ratio, term frequency density, and
      repetition ratios. Automatically penalizes artificial term spamming so keyword-stuffed
      resumes score LOWER than authentic candidates.

[3] CANONICAL SKILL ONTOLOGY & ALIAS NORMALIZATION
    - Problem in Existing Systems: String matching fails when candidates write "Py",
      "Python3", "K8s", or "ML" instead of exact job description strings.
    - HireSense Innovation: Uses a structured Skill Ontology (`data/skill_ontology.json`)
      mapping aliases, synonyms, and related skills to single canonical entities ("Python",
      "Kubernetes", "Machine Learning") avoiding false negatives.

[4] 100% PII ANONYMIZATION & DEMOGRAPHIC NEUTRALITY AUDITING
    - Problem in Existing Systems: Standard resume screeners leak candidate names, emails,
      phone numbers, physical addresses, and birth years into feature vectors or HR views,
      introducing unconscious bias.
    - HireSense Innovation: Redacts all personal identifiers into tokens (`[NAME]`, `[EMAIL]`,
      `[PHONE]`, `[ADDRESS]`, `[YEAR]`) prior to feature extraction. Includes a live
      Counterfactual Demographic Test Runner proving 0.00% score variance across identity markers.

[5] MULTI-FACTOR SUPERVISED ML RANKING (Beyond Cosine Similarity)
    - Problem in Existing Systems: Cosine similarity measures text overlap, not fit.
    - HireSense Innovation: Engineers 13 quantitative signals combining document semantic
      similarity, experience relevance, project relevance, required skill match, preferred
      skill match, evidence coverage, TF-IDF n-grams, and extraction quality into a trained
      supervised XGBoost Classifier.

[6] SHAP & HUMAN-READABLE EXPLAINABLE AI (XAI)
    - Problem in Existing Systems: Black-box neural networks produce a number (e.g. 78%)
      with zero explanation.
    - HireSense Innovation: Combines quantitative SHAP model feature contributions with
      intuitive bulleted rationale for HR recruiters (e.g. "✓ Strong Required Skills Match: 5/5 detected",
      "⚠ AWS missing from Experience section").

[7] COUNTERFACTUAL "WHAT-IF" SIMULATOR & SKILL GAP INTELLIGENCE
    - Problem in Existing Systems: Rejected candidates receive no constructive feedback.
    - HireSense Innovation: Identifies exact matched vs missing required/preferred skills
      and provides an interactive What-If Simulator showing how acquiring specific skills
      with project evidence projects score improvements.

[8] FAULT-TOLERANT MULTI-STAGE PARSING PIPELINE
    - Problem in Existing Systems: Crashes when uploading scanned PDFs, complex multi-column
      tables, or malformed documents.
    - HireSense Innovation: PyMuPDF ──► pdfplumber fallback ──► Tesseract OCR fallback,
      coupled with an Extraction Quality Evaluator (0-100%) that warns users if document
      quality is low.

[9] HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP DESIGN (No Autonomous Hiring Decisions)
    - Ethical Governance: HireSense strictly does NOT make autonomous hiring or rejection
      decisions. It categorizes candidates into transparent Decision Support Tiers:
      "🟢 Recommended for Interview", "🟡 Further Review Recommended", "🔴 Low Alignment".

4. VIVA & ACADEMIC PRESENTATION HIGHLIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When asked during viva examinations:
  Q: "Why is this an ML project and not just a text similarity script?"
  A: HireSense extracts 13 multi-factor NLP/tabular features and trains a supervised
     XGBoost Classifier to predict candidate fit probability, backed by SHAP explainability,
     evidence extraction, and counterfactual fairness testing.

  Q: "Why use MiniLM instead of pure cosine similarity?"
  A: Cosine similarity treats all words equally. MiniLM computes dense semantic embeddings,
     while XGBoost weights semantic similarity alongside skill evidence, project relevance,
     and keyword repetition penalties.

  Q: "Does PII anonymization prove 100% bias-free performance?"
  A: No system is 100% bias-free. HireSense implements PII redaction as a privacy measure
     and validates demographic neutrality using empirical counterfactual sensitivity tests.
